# Connect by ISCE — Product Knowledgebase

> For use by the ISCE Personal Assistant AI agent. Written in plain language — no technical terms.

---

## What is Connect by ISCE?

**Connect by ISCE** is a digital business card and personal networking platform. Instead of printing physical business cards, users create a digital profile that contains all their contact details, social media links, portfolio, meeting links, files, and more — all in one place.

Anyone can share their Connect profile by:

- **Tapping an NFC device** (like a smart card or wearable) to a phone
- **Scanning a QR code**
- **Sharing a link**

When someone taps or scans, they instantly see the person's full profile and can save their contact details.

---

## Who Uses Connect?

Connect is for anyone who wants to share their professional or personal identity digitally:

- Professionals networking at events
- Business owners sharing their brand
- Freelancers and creatives showing their portfolio
- Service providers sharing their booking and meeting links
- Artisans and tradespeople listing their services

---

## Core Concepts

### Profiles

A user can have **multiple profiles** on Connect — for example, one personal profile and one business profile, or separate profiles for different brands or roles. Each profile is independent and can have its own content, look, and settings.

Users can:

- Create a new profile with a name, photo, cover photo, bio, job title, location, and address
- Set one profile as their **default** (the main one that shows when someone taps their card)
- Switch between profiles easily
- Clone an existing profile to use it as a starting point for a new one
- Delete a profile (it can be restored later if needed)

---

## What Can a Profile Contain?

Each profile can have multiple sections of content. Users can add, edit, reorder, show, or hide any section.

---

### 1. Contact Information

Phone numbers associated with the profile. Each number can have a label (e.g., "Mobile", "Work", "WhatsApp"). One number can be marked as the primary contact.

---

### 2. Social Media Links

Links to any social platform — LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat, Pinterest, GitHub, Behance, Dribbble, and more. Each link has a label and can be shown or hidden.

---

### 3. Custom Links

Any URL the user wants to share — a website, blog, portfolio, store, or anything else. Each link has a title and an icon image. Users can drag them into any order they like.

---

### 4. Videos

Embedded video links from platforms like YouTube or Vimeo. One video can be marked as "featured" to appear at the top. Videos can be shown or hidden.

---

### 5. Meeting Links

Links for scheduling virtual meetings — Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Calendly, etc. One link can be set as the default meeting link. These help others book time with the profile owner instantly.

---

### 6. Appointment Links

Dedicated booking links for appointments — Calendly, Acuity, Doodle, and similar tools. One can be set as the default. Ideal for service providers, consultants, and anyone who takes bookings.

---

### 7. Spotify

Music or podcast content from Spotify — tracks, albums, playlists, or artist pages. Useful for musicians, podcasters, or anyone who wants to share what they listen to. Items can be ordered and shown or hidden.

---

### 8. Files

Documents and files the user wants to share — PDFs, portfolios, contracts, brochures, presentations, etc. Visitors can download the files. Files can be soft-deleted (hidden but kept) or permanently removed.

---

### 9. Forms

Custom forms that visitors can fill out directly from a profile. The profile owner creates the form with questions (text, email, dropdown, checkbox, etc.). Forms can be:

- **Draft** — still being built, not visible to visitors
- **Active** — live and accepting responses
- **Closed** — no longer accepting responses

Users can also create forms from pre-made templates to get started faster. Submitted responses are saved and can be reviewed by the profile owner.

---

### 10. Wallets / Crypto Addresses

Cryptocurrency wallet addresses that can be shared on a profile (e.g., Ethereum, Bitcoin, and other networks). Supports ENS names. One wallet can be set as the default. Useful for users who accept crypto payments or tips.

---

## Profile Customisation (Connect Config)

Each profile can be visually customised:

- **Theme**: Light or dark mode
- **Colours**: Primary and accent colours for the profile page
- **Branding**: A custom avatar, hero/banner image, headline text, and sub-headline text
- **Module visibility and order**: Each section (socials, links, videos, etc.) can be turned on or off, and reordered to appear in any sequence
- **Module layout**: Some sections have layout options (e.g., grid vs. list view) and a maximum number of items to show

---

## Sharing Your Profile

### NFC Tap

Connect supports NFC wearables — smart cards, wristbands, rings, or other NFC devices. When a user taps their NFC device to someone's phone, the receiver instantly sees the profile. No app required on the receiver's end.

### QR Code

Every profile has a QR code. Scanning it opens the profile in a browser.

### Direct Link

Every profile has a unique URL (slug) that can be shared anywhere — via WhatsApp, email, social media, etc.

---

## Analytics — Card Interactions

Connect tracks how many times a profile has been viewed or tapped. Profile owners can see:

- Total number of card interactions (views and taps)
- History of interactions (when and how)

This helps users understand how much engagement their digital card is getting.

---

## Saved Contacts

When someone views your Connect profile, they can save it. Profile owners can also save other people's Connect profiles as contacts. Think of it like a digital address book built from other people's Connect cards.

---

## Notifications

Connect sends push notifications to users — for things like new form submissions, new saved contacts, booking updates, and more. Users can manage their notification preferences.

---

## Support

Users can submit support requests directly from the app if they have an issue or question. These go to the ISCE support team.

---

## Artisan Feature

Connect has a special feature for **service professionals and tradespeople** called the Artisan module. An artisan is a verified service provider on the platform.

Artisans can:

- **Register as an artisan** and create a professional service profile
- **List their services** — with names, descriptions, pricing, and categories
- **Manage bookings** — clients can book the artisan's services; artisans can accept, manage, or cancel bookings
- **Track earnings** — see a summary of income from completed bookings
- **Build a portfolio** — showcase past work with images and descriptions
- **Receive reviews** — clients leave ratings and reviews after a service
- **Run promotions** — create special offers or discounts on services
- **Message clients** — a built-in messaging thread between the artisan and their clients
- **Appear in the artisan directory** — other users on the platform can discover and book artisans

The artisan profile is tied to a Connect profile, so a user's artisan services and professional digital card are connected.

---

## Devices / Wearables

Users can link physical NFC devices (cards, wristbands, etc.) to their Connect profile. When someone taps the device, it loads the linked profile. Users can manage which profiles are linked to which devices from the app.

---

## Account & Settings

Users can manage their account settings from Connect, including:

- Profile photo and personal details
- Account security
- Connected devices
- Notification preferences

---

## Multi-Profile Behaviour

- A user can have many profiles under one account
- Each profile is independent with its own content and settings
- One profile is set as the **default** — this is what shows when using a linked NFC device without specifying a profile
- Any content item (link, social, video, etc.) can be individually shown or hidden without deleting it
- Most deleted items are soft-deleted (hidden but recoverable), not permanently gone
