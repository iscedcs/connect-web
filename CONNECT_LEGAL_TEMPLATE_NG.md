# Connect Service – Legal Documentation Template (Nigeria)

> **Disclaimer (Non-Legal Advice)**
> This document is a **template and briefing aid** to help your legal counsel draft a formal **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** for the **Connect** product within your wider ecosystem (Authentication, Connect, Events). It **does not constitute legal advice**. You must have a qualified lawyer admitted in **Nigeria** review and adapt this for your specific business, data flows, and risk profile.

---

## 1. Context and Scope

-   **Ecosystem**: The business operates a larger platform with at least three core components:
    -   **Authentication Service** – identity, login, security, session management.
    -   **Connect Service (this product)** – profile creation, contact sharing, digital cards, links, files, and social integrations.
    -   **Events Service** – event pages, RSVPs, ticketing or guest management, and event-related communications.
-   **Territory Focus**: Primary operations or target users include **Nigeria**. The service may be accessible globally but is anchored in **Nigerian law**, especially for data protection.
-   **Intended Documents**:
    -   Part A – Outline for **Terms of Service (ToS)** specific to Connect but aligned with the wider ecosystem.
    -   Part B – Outline for a **Privacy Policy** reflecting Nigerian data protection law and cross-service data flows.

Legal team should use this as:

-   A **requirements checklist** to ensure all necessary clauses are covered.
-   A **mapping tool** between technical behaviour (how Connect works) and legal language (what is disclosed and agreed).

---

## 2. Key Definitions (for Legal Draft)

Legal counsel can refine wording, but typical definitions to include:

-   **“Company”, “we”, “us”, “our”** – The legal entity operating the Authentication, Connect, and Events services.
-   **“Platform”** – The entire ecosystem, including the Authentication, Connect, and Events components, delivered via web, mobile, NFC, and other interfaces.
-   **“Connect Service” / “Connect”** – The part of the Platform that allows users to create and manage digital profiles, share contact information, store or share links, files, and social handles, and manage how this information is presented to others.
-   **“User”, “you”, “your”** – Any person who accesses or uses the Platform, including:
    -   **Account holders** (authenticated users) and
    -   **Recipients/visitors** who access public or semi-public Connect profiles or event pages.
-   **“Profile Data”** – Personal and professional information the user chooses to add to their Connect profile (name, photo, contact details, links, social handles, biography, company information, etc.).
-   **“Authentication Data”** – Credentials and identifiers managed by the Authentication Service (email, phone number, password hashes, identity tokens, multi-factor authentication data, device identifiers, security logs).
-   **“Event Data”** – Information associated with Events (event name, location, time, invitations, attendance, RSVPs, ticket references, check-in data).
-   **“Personal Data” / “Personal Information”** – Any information that relates to an identified or identifiable natural person, as interpreted under Nigerian law.
-   **“Controller” / “Data Controller”** – The entity determining the purposes and means of processing Personal Data under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)** and the **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)**.
-   **“Processor” / “Data Processor”** – Any third party that processes Personal Data on behalf of the Company (e.g., cloud providers, analytics, payment gateways, communications vendors).

## Part A – Terms of Service Outline (Connect, Nigeria)

This section is a structured checklist for the **Terms of Service** applicable to the Connect Service within the wider ecosystem.

### A1. Introduction and Agreement

-   Short description of the **Connect Service** and how it fits into the overall Platform.
-   Statement that by **accessing or using Connect**, the user agrees to be bound by the ToS and any ecosystem-wide terms.
-   Clarify relationship between:
    -   **General Platform Terms** (for authentication and common rules).
    -   **Connect-specific terms** (this document).
    -   **Events-specific terms** (if separate).
-   Links to related documents: Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy (if separate), and Events ToS.

### A2. Eligibility and Account Requirements

-   Minimum age requirement (e.g., 18+ or as permitted by Nigerian law; handle 13–17 with guardian consent if applicable).
-   Requirement to provide accurate, complete account information during registration (handled by Authentication Service).
-   Responsibility for maintaining the confidentiality of login credentials; tie-in to Authentication terms.
-   Statement that accounts may be created via email, phone OTP, social login, or enterprise SSO where available.

### A3. Description of the Connect Service

-   Core features, for example:
    -   Create, update, and delete one or more **Connect profiles**.
    -   Add contact channels (phone, email, messaging handles).
    -   Add company details, job titles, and descriptions.
    -   Attach or link to files (e.g., brochures, portfolios, PDFs).
    -   Add external links (websites, social media, payment links, scheduling links).
    -   Connect to NFC cards, wearables, QR codes, or similar devices.
    -   Share profiles with others and control visibility (public, private, link-only, per-event, etc.).
-   Reference that some features may depend on or interact with the **Events Service** (e.g., using a Connect profile as an event check-in identity).
-   Clarify that features may change, be added, or removed, with notice where required.

### A4. User Obligations and Acceptable Use

-   Prohibited behaviours in Connect and the wider Platform, such as:
    -   Using Connect for unlawful, fraudulent, or harmful activities under Nigerian law.
    -   Sharing misleading or false identity data, impersonation, or unauthorised representation of another person or business.
    -   Uploading or sharing content that is defamatory, obscene, hateful, or violates third-party rights (IP, privacy, publicity).
    -   Uploading malware or using Connect to attack or probe systems.
-   Rules for profile naming, branding, and representing companies or organisations (e.g., must have authority to use a company’s name or logo).
-   Responsibilities relating to invitations, event networking, and sharing other people’s contact details (user must have consent to share third-party personal data).

### A5. Content Ownership and Licences

-   Clarify that **users retain ownership** of the content and Profile Data they upload to Connect.
-   Grant to the Company a **limited, non-exclusive, worldwide licence** to host, display, and process content strictly to operate, improve, and secure the Platform.
-   Define what is considered **Company Materials** (software, branding, logos, design, documentation) and state that users receive only a limited, revocable licence to use the Platform.
-   Clarify IP relating to templates, widgets, integrations, and designs.

### A6. Third-Party Services and Integrations

-   Describe integration with third-party services such as:
    -   Social networks (e.g., LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, etc.).
    -   Scheduling tools (e.g., Calendly-like integrations).
    -   File storage or CDN providers.
    -   Analytics and communication tools.
-   Clarify that third-party services have **their own terms and privacy policies**.
-   State that the Company is not responsible for third-party services and that use of them is at the user’s option and risk.

### A7. Plans, Fees, and Payments (if applicable)

-   Distinguish between **free** and **paid** tiers, if offered.
-   Describe any subscription plans, billing cycles, and renewal logic.
-   Payment processing via third-party processors; refer to separate payment terms where appropriate.
-   Outline refund policy, if any, consistent with Nigerian consumer protection requirements.

### A8. Availability, Support, and Modifications

-   State that Connect is provided on an **“as is” and “as available”** basis (subject to any mandatory Nigerian consumer law).
-   Right to **modify, suspend, or discontinue** parts of the service, with reasonable notice where feasible.
-   High-level SLAs or support channels (email, in-app support, helpdesk).
-   Maintenance windows, beta features, and feature flags.

### A9. Security Responsibilities

-   Commit to implementing **reasonable technical and organisational security measures** (with more detail in the Privacy Policy).
-   User obligations:
    -   Use strong passwords and update them regularly.
    -   Report suspected unauthorised access promptly.
    -   Ensure their devices and browsers are secure and updated.
-   Cross-reference the ecosystem’s **Security and Privacy** documentation.

### A10. Limitation of Liability and Indemnity

-   Clearly state any **limitations of liability** permitted under Nigerian law (e.g., caps on damages, exclusions for indirect or consequential losses).
-   Acknowledge that nothing in the ToS seeks to exclude liability where doing so is prohibited by applicable Nigerian law (e.g., fraud, wilful misconduct).
-   Include any **indemnity clause** where users agree to indemnify the Company against claims arising from:
    -   Violation of the ToS or laws,
    -   Infringement of third-party rights through use of Connect,
    -   Misuse of Events or Connect in a corporate setting.

### A11. Termination and Suspension

-   Conditions under which the Company may **suspend** or **terminate** a user’s access to Connect or the whole Platform (e.g., repeated ToS violations, legal compliance, security risks).
-   Effect of termination on:
    -   Access to the user’s Profiles, Events, and data.
    -   Retention of data for legal or legitimate business purposes (cross-reference Privacy Policy).
    -   Any continuing payment obligations or outstanding invoices.

### A12. Governing Law, Jurisdiction, and Dispute Resolution

-   State that the ToS are governed by the **laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria**.
-   Identify the primary jurisdiction and courts (e.g., **courts of Lagos State, Nigeria**), unless the Company chooses another Nigerian jurisdiction.
-   Optionally include:
    -   **Internal complaint or escalation procedure** before formal action.
    -   **Mediation or arbitration** clause (e.g., Lagos Multi-Door Courthouse or similar) before court proceedings, if aligned with company policy.
-   For cross-border users, clarify any conflict-of-law considerations.

### A13. Changes to the Terms

-   State the Company’s right to update the ToS for Connect and the overall Platform.
-   Describe how users will be notified (email, in-app notifications, website notice).
-   Specify the **effective date** and the user’s right to stop using the service if they do not agree with the changes.

## Part B – Privacy Policy Outline (Nigeria, Connect-Focused)

This section maps the technical behaviour of the Authentication, Connect, and Events services to what should be disclosed in a Nigeria-compliant **Privacy Policy**.

### B1. Data Controller and Contact Details

-   Identify the **legal entity** acting as **Data Controller** for Nigerian users.
-   Provide contact details for:
    -   General privacy enquiries (email, postal address).
    -   **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** or privacy lead, if appointed, as recommended under NDPA.
-   If separate entities operate the Authentication, Connect, and Events services, clarify joint controllership or separate controllership relationships.

### B2. Applicable Law and Supervisory Authority

-   Explicitly reference applicable data protection laws, for example:
    -   **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)**.
    -   **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)** and any relevant implementation guidelines.
-   Identify the main supervisory authority:
    -   **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)** or its current official designation (confirm latest name/structure with counsel).
-   Mention that data subjects in Nigeria may lodge complaints with this authority.

### B3. Categories of Personal Data Collected

Structure by **source/service** so it is clear where data comes from.

1. **Authentication Data**

    - Identifiers: email, phone number, username, internal user IDs.
    - Credentials: password hashes, OTP verification data, MFA tokens.
    - Login logs: timestamps, IP address, device or browser information, approximate location.

2. **Connect Profile Data**

    - Basic identity: full name, profile photo, gender (if collected), job title, employer, biography.
    - Contact details: phone numbers, email addresses, messaging handles.
    - Professional details: company name, department, address, business registration identifiers (if collected).
    - Online presence: websites, social links, meeting links, payment links.
    - Files: uploaded documents, images, videos, or other media attached to profiles.
    - Preferences: visibility settings, sharing preferences, saved templates, language settings.

3. **Events-Related Data (where linked to Connect)**

    - RSVPs, attendance records, ticket or invitation identifiers.
    - Event-specific tags (e.g., exhibitor, speaker, attendee).
    - Interactions at events such as check-ins using a Connect profile or NFC card.

4. **Usage and Device Data**

    - Logs of how users interact with Connect (pages viewed, features used, clickstream events).
    - Device, browser, and network information (operating system, browser version, IP address, approximate location).
    - Crash logs and performance metrics.

5. **Marketing and Communication Data**

    - Communication preferences (email/SMS/WhatsApp/other channels).
    - Records of messages sent (e.g., onboarding emails, transactional messages, event reminders).
    - Marketing consents and opt-outs.

6. **Special Categories and Sensitive Data**
    - State explicitly **whether you collect any special categories of personal data** under Nigerian law (e.g., health, religion, biometric data).
    - If you use **biometric features** for authentication or identification at events, this must be very clearly disclosed and justified.

### B4. Sources of Personal Data

-   Direct collection from users via:
    -   Account creation and profile editing forms.
    -   File uploads and content creation in Connect.
    -   RSVPs and event interactions.
-   Indirect collection from:
    -   Third-party integrations (social networks, calendars, CRMs).
    -   Other users who share contact or event information involving the data subject.
    -   Public sources, if applicable (e.g., public business registries or websites).

### B5. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

For each **purpose**, specify:
(a) what data is used, (b) which legal basis is relied on (e.g., consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest).

Examples:

1. **Account creation and authentication**

    - Data: Authentication Data, basic identity.
    - Legal basis: **performance of a contract** (providing the Platform) and **legitimate interest** (ensuring account security).

2. **Provision and personalisation of Connect profiles**

    - Data: Connect Profile Data, usage data.
    - Legal basis: **performance of a contract** (providing Connect) and **legitimate interest** (improving user experience).

3. **Event participation and networking**

    - Data: Profile Data, Event Data, usage data.
    - Legal basis: **performance of a contract** (event participation) and, where applicable, **consent** (e.g., when sharing attendee lists or making profiles discoverable).

4. **Security, fraud prevention, and abuse detection**

    - Data: Authentication logs, usage data, device data.
    - Legal basis: **legitimate interest** in protecting the Platform, users, and the Company; and **legal obligation** where required (e.g., record-keeping, law enforcement requests).

5. **Analytics and product improvement**

    - Data: Aggregated usage data, device data.
    - Legal basis: **legitimate interest** in understanding and improving services; ensure opt-out or minimisation options where appropriate.

6. **Marketing and communications**
    - Data: contact details, usage data, communication preferences.
    - Legal basis: **consent** for electronic marketing where required; **legitimate interest** for certain service-related communications.
    - Explain users’ right to **opt out** of direct marketing at any time.

### B6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

-   Categories of third-party recipients, for example:
    -   **Infrastructure providers** (cloud hosting, CDN, backup).
    -   **Analytics and monitoring tools**.
    -   **Communications providers** (email, SMS, push notifications, WhatsApp or similar).
    -   **Payment processors and financial institutions** (if you sell premium plans or tickets).
    -   **Verification providers** (e.g., BVN verification partners, KYC services) if applicable; cross-reference [schemas/bvn.ts](schemas/bvn.ts) where relevant to product behaviour.
-   Circumstances under which Personal Data may be shared with:
    -   **Event organisers** using the Events Service.
    -   **Business customers** using Connect in a corporate setting.
-   Disclosures required by law or for legal claims (e.g., to law enforcement, regulators, courts).

### B7. International Data Transfers

-   Clarify where data is stored and processed (e.g., within Nigeria, elsewhere in Africa, EU, US, etc.).
-   Where data is transferred outside Nigeria, explain:
    -   The **countries or regions** involved (at least by category).
    -   The **safeguards** used (e.g., contractual clauses, data protection agreements).
-   Confirm alignment with cross-border transfer requirements under NDPA/NDPR.

### B8. Data Retention

-   Provide a **clear retention policy** for each major data category:
    -   Authentication logs.
    -   Connect Profile Data and uploaded files.
    -   Event Data and related audit logs.
-   Explain that retention is based on:
    -   Duration of account or service usage.
    -   Legal obligations (e.g., tax, accounting, regulatory).
    -   Legitimate interests (e.g., fraud prevention, dispute resolution) with specified retention limits.
-   Explain what happens when users **delete profiles** or **close accounts** (e.g., anonymisation vs. deletion, backups, and logs).

### B9. Users’ Rights (Data Subject Rights)

-   Outline rights available to Nigerian data subjects under NDPA/NDPR, including:
    -   Right to **access** their personal data.
    -   Right to **rectification** of inaccurate or incomplete data.
    -   Right to **erasure** (subject to legal and legitimate-interest exemptions).
    -   Right to **object** to certain processing (e.g., direct marketing, profiling).
    -   Right to **data portability** (where technically feasible).
    -   Right to **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
-   Explain the **procedure** for exercising rights:
    -   Contact method (email, in-app request form).
    -   Identity verification steps.
    -   Standard response timeframes.

### B10. Security Measures

-   High-level description of the technical and organisational measures used, such as:
    -   Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest where applicable.
    -   Access controls, role-based permissions, and audit logging.
    -   Regular security testing, vulnerability management, and monitoring.
    -   Vendor due diligence and data processing agreements.
-   Clarify that no system is perfectly secure but that reasonable safeguards are maintained.

### B11. Children’s Data

-   State whether Connect is **designed for or knowingly used by children** below a defined age (e.g., under 18).
-   If you allow minors to use the Platform, include:
    -   Conditions for **parental or guardian consent**.
    -   Specific protections and limitations on profiling and marketing.
-   If the service is not intended for children, state that you do not knowingly collect children’s data and describe what happens if such data is discovered.

### B12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

-   State whether and how you use:
    -   Cookies (session, persistent).
    -   Local storage or similar browser technologies.
    -   Analytics tags, pixels, and SDKs.
-   Explain:
    -   The **purposes** of these technologies (authentication, security, preferences, analytics, marketing).
    -   Opt-out or control mechanisms for users (browser settings, cookie banners, in-app toggles).

### B13. Changes to the Privacy Policy

-   Explain how users will be notified of any material changes to the Privacy Policy.
-   Indicate where the **current version** can always be found.
-   Provide an effective date and, where relevant, summary of key changes.

### B14. Complaints and Contact Information

-   Describe how users can **raise complaints** or concerns directly with the Company.
-   Provide a clear email address or web form link for privacy-related issues.
-   Inform users of their right to lodge a complaint with the relevant **Nigerian data protection authority**.
